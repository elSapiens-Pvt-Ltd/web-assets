The CRAFT Method
5 elements that turn AI from mediocre to magical.
C
Context
Who you are, what you do, who you're talking to
R
Role
"Act as a marketing expert / lawyer / HR manager..."
A
Action
Write, summarise, compare, plan, analyse — be specific
F
Format
Bullet list, email, table, 150 words, 3 paragraphs
T
Tone
Professional, warm, urgent, bold, casual — AI nails it
ai_prompt.sh
$ "Write a follow-up email after my BNI 1-to-1."
→ Generic. Could be anyone. Won't send it.
# ─────────── CRAFT version ───────────
$ "I'm a financial advisor at BNI Falcon. [C]
 Act as a warm relationship-builder. [R]
 Write a 100-word follow-up after a 1-to-1 [A]
 where we explored a referral partnership.
 Format: subject + 2 paras + CTA. [F]
 Tone: professional but personal." [T]
OUTPUT:
Subject: Great connecting today — here's what I had in mind...
Hi Priya, it was wonderful meeting you this morning at our chapter meeting. I've been thinking about how a referral partnership between us could genuinely help both our clients...
→ Ready to send. Sounds like you. Gets replies.
